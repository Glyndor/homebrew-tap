# The entries of scripts/quality/lint/switch-rule-forms.json that are of each channel repository, left out of the
# comparison between the two: 'section<TAB>workflow<TAB>job'. Everything else in that file has to be the same in both.
# common-switch-forms.sh refuses a line under which the two copies say the same. This file is a shared copy: both
# repositories leave out the same entries.
#
# The report of what the app pushed waits for the checks of each channel: formulae and style in the tap, formulae being
# manifests in the bucket.
conditional_jobs	.github/workflows/generated-on-main.yml	report
# The update of the tap also audits on macOS what it rendered, and its commit waits for that; the bucket has no such job.
conditional_jobs	.github/workflows/update.yml	commit
conditional_jobs	.github/workflows/update.yml	validate-macos
step_conditions	.github/workflows/update.yml	validate-macos
# The audit of the tap waits for the job that decides whether the change can affect it, and the gate answers for that
# audit; the bucket has no audit of that shape, so the entries of those two jobs are of the tap alone.
conditional_jobs	.github/workflows/audit.yml	audit
conditional_jobs	.github/workflows/audit.yml	gate
# The update of the tap validates what it rendered; the bucket has no job of that shape, so the entry of that job is of
# the tap alone.
conditional_jobs	.github/workflows/update.yml	validate
step_conditions	.github/workflows/update.yml	validate
# The tap runs `brew style` as a job of tests.yml; the bucket has no such job, so the entry of that job is of the tap
# alone.
step_conditions	.github/workflows/tests.yml	formulae
# The bucket runs the manifests in a job of tests.yml; the tap has no such job, so the entry of that job is of the
# bucket alone.
step_conditions	.github/workflows/tests.yml	manifests
